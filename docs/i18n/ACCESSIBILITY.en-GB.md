# Prospero.Eden Encore — Accessibility (English UK)

[All 31 PS5 language choices](../../LANGUAGES.md) · [English US complete reference](../../ACCESSIBILITY.md) · [PS5 language policy](../PS5_SYSTEM_LANGUAGE_POLICY.md)

The launcher documents larger text, high contrast, reduced motion and TV navigation. Move with the DualSense D-pad, choose with Cross and go back with Circle. Per-game and global controller mappings can be customised, but the PS button remains the PS5 system's reserved control.

A short touchpad click opens the native Vulkan quick overlay with graphics, button mapping, FPS, diagnostic switches and a Multiplayer shortcut. This shortcut does not embed another real-time lobby: it requires a second confirmation and then **ends the game** before opening the original room browser. The launcher provides exactly 31 PS5 language catalogues, including separate British and American English; complex-script legibility needs PS5 device validation.
