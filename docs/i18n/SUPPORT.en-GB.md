# Prospero.Eden Encore — Support (English UK)

[All 31 PS5 language choices](../../LANGUAGES.md) · [English US complete reference](../../SUPPORT.md) · [PS5 language policy](../PS5_SYSTEM_LANGUAGE_POLICY.md)

For a useful bug report include your release SHA, firmware, renderer, game stage, minimal reproduction and a bounded, redacted diagnostic log. Remove keys, saved files, tokens, account identifiers and private details from attachments. Back up `/data/prosperoeden/` before installing or resetting anything.

For English (UK), report incorrect regional text such as Color rather than Colour or Customize rather than Customise with the affected screen and exact string. The two English catalogues are distinct among the 31 PS5 system choices. Real network room joining and local wireless remain subject to console validation; the quick overlay only navigates to the original launcher lobby after ending the game.
